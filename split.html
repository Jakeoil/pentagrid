The grid on the left, its dual on the right — one γ, one view

The same pentagrid, drawn twice. On the left, what de Bruijn starts from: lines, the regions between them, the crossings. On the right, what the dual map makes of it: vertices, edges, tiles. Pan or zoom either and the other follows; the offsets are shared, so the reticulum and the presets drive both. Each side keeps its own switches.
